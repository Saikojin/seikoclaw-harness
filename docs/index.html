Section 05 · Adversarial Stress Audit

8 bugs uncovered and fixed via break-ui

Every component looks clean when tested against short demo strings. Feeding worst-case realistic data through WORST_CASE_EQUIPMENT exposed 8 bugs that were fixed and locked into src/test/BreakUi.test.tsx.

# Severity Component & Field Worst-Case Value What Broke Before Fix Fix Applied
1 BROKEN Catalog.tsx (maxPrice) $24,995 & $129,999 commercial rigs Slider max was 5000 ($5,000+), silently filtering out all items over $5,000 on initial page load Treated maxPrice === 5000 as unbounded ($5,000+) in Catalog.tsx:73
2 UGLY EquipmentCard.tsx (badge-savings) Pre-owned card with Save 30% pill Top-left flex-wrap row stretched horizontally into the top-right 98/100 Inspected badge Stacked top-left badges vertically with flex-col items-start gap-1.5 max-w-[58%]
3 BROKEN EquipmentCard.tsx (images[0]) https://invalid.example.test/... Browser rendered broken image icon with collapsed container contrast Added imgError state & fallback panel (Verified Equipment Photo Pending)
4 UGLY EquipmentCard.tsx (location.city) Kleinfeltersville-Schwenksville, PA Compound city name wrapped the bottom image overlay banner onto two lines Added min-w-0 truncate on location and flex-shrink-0 on freight cost
5 UGLY EquipmentCard.tsx (brand) Hammer Strength by Life Fitness Commercial Division 52-char brand wrapped onto 2 lines, misaligning card titles across the 3-column grid Added truncate and full title tooltip
6 UGLY EquipmentCard.tsx (price) $129,999 + $185,000 MSRP Six-figure price + strikethrough MSRP squished the Add to Cart and Details buttons Added tabular-nums, min-w-0 on price column, and flex-shrink-0 on CTAs
7 UGLY Catalog.tsx (results-count) 1 item dataset mode Rendered ungrammatical "1 Equipment Listings Found" Added singular/plural check (1 Equipment Listing Found)
