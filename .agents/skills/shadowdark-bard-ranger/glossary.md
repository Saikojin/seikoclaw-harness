# Glossary of Terms

### Bardic Arts
A core Bard feature granting passive Advantage on all ability checks regarding oration, performing arts, lore, and diplomacy.

### Curative
An advanced herbal remedy (DC 15 Intelligence check) that duplicates the healing properties of a standard Potion of Healing (1d6 HP).

### Foebane
A combat-oriented herbal remedy (DC 13 Intelligence check) granting Advantage on both attack and damage rolls against a designated creature type for 1d6 rounds.

### Luck Token
A meta-currency granted to a target via Bard's Inspire effect; allows a player to reroll any d20 check.

### Magical Dabbler
A Bard feature allowing the activation of priest or wizard spell scrolls and wands using Charisma as the spellcasting modifier; critical failures provoke a wizard mishap.

### Near
Shadowdark standard distance band (typically within ~30 feet, reachable with a single movement action).

### Presence
The Bard's active supernatural aura system; enacted by rolling a DC 12 (or DC 9 with talent) Charisma check.

### Restorative
A medicinal herbal remedy (DC 14 Intelligence check) that cleanses one active poison or disease condition.

### Salve
The foundational herbal remedy (DC 11 Intelligence check) restoring 1 hit point to a wounded creature.

### Stimulant
An herbal remedy (DC 12 Intelligence check) providing total immunity to surprise for 10 rounds.

### Wayfinder
A core Ranger feature granting constant Advantage on navigation, tracking, bushcraft, stealth, and wild animal checks.
