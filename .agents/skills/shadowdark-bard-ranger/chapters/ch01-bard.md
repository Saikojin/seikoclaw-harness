# Chapter 1: The Bard Class

Bards are welcome wanderers and wise advisors; it is their task to protect and share knowledge handed down through the ages.

## Class Fundamentals
- **Hit Points**: 1d6 per level
- **Weapons**: Crossbow, dagger, mace, shortbow, shortsword, spear, staff
- **Armor**: Leather armor, chainmail, shields
- **Languages**: 4 additional common languages and 1 rare language.

## Core Features & Abilities

### Bardic Arts
Trained in oration, performing arts, lore, and diplomacy.
- **Effect**: You have **Advantage** on all related checks.

### Magical Dabbler
You can activate spell scrolls and wands using **Charisma** as your spellcasting stat.
- **Risk**: If you critically fail, you must roll on the **wizard mishap** table.

### Presence
Make a **DC 12 Charisma check** to enact one of the following effects:
- **Inspire**: One target within *near* gains a **luck token**.
- **Fascinate (Focus)**: You transfix all chosen targets of level 4 or less within *near*.
- **Failure Constraint**: If you fail to enact an effect (excluding focus), you cannot use that effect again until you complete a rest.

### Prolific
- Add **1d6** to your learning rolls.
- Groups carousing with 1 or more bards add **1d6** to their carousing rolls.

## Bard Talents (2d6 Table)

Roll 2d6 when advancing:

| 2d6 | Talent Effect |
| :---: | :--- |
| **2** | You find a random priest or wizard wand (your choice). |
| **3–6** | +1 to melee and ranged attacks **OR** +1 to Magical Dabbler rolls. |
| **7–9** | +2 points to distribute to any stats. |
| **10–11** | Your Presence effects become **DC 9** to enact (duplicate rolls reroll). |
| **12** | Choose a talent. |
