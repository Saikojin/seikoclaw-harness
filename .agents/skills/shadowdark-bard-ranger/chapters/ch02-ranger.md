# Chapter 2: The Ranger Class

Skilled trackers, stealthy wanderers, and peerless warriors who call the wilds their home.

## Class Fundamentals
- **Hit Points**: 1d8 per level
- **Weapons**: Dagger, longbow, longsword, shortbow, shortsword, spear, staff
- **Armor**: Leather armor, chainmail (no shields)

## Core Features & Abilities

### Wayfinder
You have **Advantage** on all checks associated with:
- Navigation
- Tracking
- Bushcraft
- Stealth
- Wild animals

### Herbalism
Make an **Intelligence check** to prepare an herbal remedy of your choice.
- **Rules & Limits**:
  - If you fail the check, you cannot attempt that specific remedy again until you successfully complete a rest.
  - **Expiry**: Unused remedies expire in **3 rounds**.

#### Herbal Remedy Recipes

| DC | Remedy | Effect |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **11** | **Salve** | Heals **1 HP**. |
| **12** | **Stimulant** | Target cannot be surprised for **10 rounds**. |
| **13** | **Foebane** | Gain **Advantage on attacks and damage** against one chosen creature type for **1d6 rounds**. |
| **14** | **Restorative** | Ends one active poison or disease. |
| **15** | **Curative** | Functions as equivalent to a **Potion of Healing**. |

## Ranger Talents (2d6 Table)

Roll 2d6 when advancing:

| 2d6 | Talent Effect |
| :---: | :--- |
| **2** | You deal **d12 damage** with one weapon type of your choice. |
| **3–6** | +1 to melee or ranged attacks and damage. |
| **7–9** | +2 to Strength, Dexterity, or Intelligence stat. |
| **10–11** | You gain Advantage on Herbalism checks for one chosen remedy. |
| **12** | Choose a talent **OR** +2 points to distribute to stats. |
