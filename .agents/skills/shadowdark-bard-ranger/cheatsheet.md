# Cheatsheet & Quick Decision Reference

## Difficulty Classes (DC) & Triggers

### Bard Presence (Charisma Check)
- **Base DC**: 12 (Reduced to DC 9 with Talent 10–11).
- **Inspire**: Target in *near* gets a Luck Token.
- **Fascinate**: Target $\le$ level 4 in *near* is transfixed (Requires Focus).
- *Failure*: Cannot use that effect again until rested.

### Ranger Herbalism (Intelligence Check)
*Unused remedies expire in 3 rounds. Failure locks out that remedy until next rest.*

| DC | Remedy | Mechanical Trigger & Output |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **11** | Salve | Restore 1 HP |
| **12** | Stimulant | Immune to surprise for 10 rounds |
| **13** | Foebane | Advantage on attacks + damage vs. chosen creature type (1d6 rounds) |
| **14** | Restorative | Cleanses 1 poison or disease |
| **15** | Curative | Potion of Healing effect (1d6 HP) |

## Advantage Matrix

| Condition | Class | Check / Roll |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Oration, Performing Arts, Lore, Diplomacy | **Bard** | Skill check |
| Navigation, Tracking, Bushcraft, Stealth, Wild Animals | **Ranger** | Skill check |
| Chosen Creature Type Combat (Foebane active) | **Ranger** | Attack roll & Damage roll |
| Chosen Remedy Preparation (Talent 10–11) | **Ranger** | Herbalism check |

## Advancement Dice (2d6) Comparison

| Roll (2d6) | Bard Advancement | Ranger Advancement |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **2** | Random wand (priest or wizard) | d12 weapon damage die (chosen type) |
| **3–6** | +1 melee/ranged attack OR +1 Magical Dabbler | +1 melee/ranged attack & damage |
| **7–9** | +2 stats distributed | +2 STR, DEX, or INT |
| **10–11** | Presence DC becomes 9 | ADV on chosen Herbalism remedy |
| **12** | Choose a talent | Choose a talent OR +2 stats |
