# Mechanics & Decision Patterns

## 1. Rest Lockout Pattern
Both Bard Presence and Ranger Herbalism enforce a per-ability failure lockout:
- If a check fails, only *that specific effect/remedy* is locked out.
- The lockout persists until the character successfully completes a rest.
- Other Presence effects or other Herbalism recipes remain usable.

## 2. Temporal Constraints & Expiry
- **Herbalism remedies**: Must be utilized within **3 rounds** of preparation or they spoil/expire.
- **Foebane**: Duration is rolled immediately upon application (**1d6 rounds**).
- **Stimulant**: Fixed duration (**10 rounds**).

## 3. High-Risk / High-Reward Activation
- **Magical Dabbler**: Enables Bards to activate spell scrolls and wands without wizard or priest multiclassing.
- **Mishap Trigger**: A natural 1 or critical failure on the Charisma check triggers an immediate roll on the wizard mishap table, exposing the party to dangerous magical backfires.

## 4. Social & Exploration Advantage Stacking
- Bards hold constant Advantage on all 4 social/lore pillars (Oration, Performance, Lore, Diplomacy).
- Rangers hold constant Advantage on all 5 exploration/survival pillars (Navigation, Tracking, Bushcraft, Stealth, Wild Animals).
- In Tablebuddy VTT or autonomous GM loops, any action tagged with these concepts automatically applies the Advantage modifier (`2d20kh1`).
